How to read this page

One row per person — 664 people, the union of the 119th Congress (540 served) and the incoming 120th (535 named). Career columns cover FEC cycles %%CAREER_START%%–%%CAREER_END%%. “Total accepted” is the headline career figure: individual contributions + corporate PAC money + AIPAC PAC direct. Everything else is shown separately and never added into it.

Definitions

  • Individual contributions — itemized + unitemized contributions from individuals to the person’s campaign(s) (FEC weball TTL_INDIV_CONTRIB).
  • Corporate PAC — direct contributions from corporate-connected PACs: committee type Q/N and org type C/W in the same cycle’s committee-master file. Trade, labor, membership, cooperative and hybrid PACs are excluded; they live in the “Other committees” view.
  • AIPAC PAC direct (headline) — total Schedule B disbursements from the AIPAC PAC (C00797670), %%IE_SPAN%%: the PAC’s own money plus earmarked individual checks transmitted through it. The earmark part is broken out separately — subtract it to get PAC own-money ($7,376,200 career vs $7,567,773 headline). Earmarked money is also inside the individual-contributions column (it is individuals’ money); never add the two columns together.
  • UDP support / benefit — United Democracy Project (C00799031) independent expenditures, %%IE_SPAN%%. Support is spending for the candidate; benefit adds opposition credit — UDP spending against a candidate’s opponent, credited only when UDP backed exactly one candidate in that race. This is money spent about a race, never given to the campaign, and it is never part of total accepted. UDP spending against a person is never netted; it is noted on their row.
  • Self-funding — candidate contributions + candidate loans (weball). Top: Rick Scott $89.99M, Darrell Issa $27.88M, David McCormick $22.09M, Pete Ricketts $19.44M, Shri Thanedar $17.46M, Anthony Constantino $17.00M.
  • Topline — total receipts and disbursements (career sums), cash on hand and debts owed (latest cycle’s value — a stock, not a sum). Debts owed to the committee are null for everyone: no weball column carries them.
  • Other committees — contributions from committees that are not corporate PACs and not the AIPAC PAC, classified by committee type: party, leadership, labor, trade, membership, cooperative, unclassified. Career total $1,568,735,747.
  • All independent expenditures — every registered committee’s spending for/against the person, %%IE_SPAN%% only (the 2010–2020 IE files are not staged). Career total $3,842,257,481 (support $1.49B / oppose $2.35B). Filtered to registered committees + identified candidates: IE files contain tens of billions in bogus filings, and the excluded amounts are documented in methodology.md §4/§8.

Zero corporate PAC money, zero AIPAC PAC direct money, and zero UDP benefit in the %%CYCLE%% cycle. %%CLEAN_TOTAL_120%% verified clean in the incoming 120th: %%HOUSE_CLEAN%% House (%%HOUSE_CLEAN_D%% D, %%HOUSE_CLEAN_R%% R) + %%SEN_CLEAN%% Senate (%%SEN_CLEAN_D%% D, %%SEN_CLEAN_I%% I, %%SEN_CLEAN_R%% R). %%CLEAN_HOUSE_R_SENTENCE%% A member is unverified only if they have no 2026 entries in any money source — %%UNVERIFIED_SENTENCE%% %%UNOPPOSED_SENTENCE%% %%CAREER_CLEAN_HOUSE%% incoming House members are career-clean (no corporate PAC, AIPAC direct, UDP support, or UDP opposition credit in any cycle).

Corporate PAC tolerance presets

Dirty money always includes any AIPAC PAC direct dollar and any UDP benefit dollar — that flag (%%FLAG_120%% of the incoming 120th) is not tolerance-based and is always on. Corporate PAC money counts as dirty under a tolerance preset: Strict — any net dirty dollar above $0 is dirty (the verified-clean count is in the paragraph above); Balanced (default) — corporate PAC over $100,000 and over 5% of the cycle’s receipts (%%BALANCED_NOT_DIRTY%% of 535 not dirty); Lenient — corporate PAC over $250,000 and over 10% of the cycle’s receipts (%%LENIENT_NOT_DIRTY%% of 535 not dirty). The middle tier — “Below threshold” — took corporate PAC money below the preset’s thresholds; it is never called clean, and the dollar amount is always shown. The full definitions are in methodology.md §14, which wins if this page ever disagrees.

Known limitations — read before quoting a number

  • %%CYCLE%% is partial (see cutoffs above). Do not compare %%CYCLE%% figures head-to-head with full cycles.
  • Elaine Luria is unverified, not clean and not dirty — her real %%CYCLE%% campaign (H6VA02198, $8.04M receipts) is unlinked; linking it is a roster change.
  • Melissa Bean and Hilda Solis show $0 individual contributions in %%CYCLE%% alongside real corporate PAC money — a missing-committee-linkage gap, not a real zero.
  • Alan Armstrong (appointed senator, never ran) has no FEC record: all money cells read “unknown,” not zero, and he is unranked.
  • All-IE “career” means %%IE_SPAN%% only. The 2010–2020 IE files are not staged.
  • Weball is a lower bound where the FEC API runs ahead (Constantino: weball loans $14.4M vs API $17M). The freshness layer closes that gap for late money.
  • Weball double-count corrected: where weball repeats an identical individual-contribution total under a chamber-switcher’s House and Senate IDs in the same cycle, it is counted once ($451,776,015.34 across 32 persons).
  • UDP reconciliation: the deduped 2024 UDP total is $38,044,932.69 vs OpenSecrets’ $37,860,200 (+0.49%, unresolved).
  • 16 people who served in the 119th but left before Nov 2026 are excluded (Vance, Rubio, Graham, D. Scott, Turner, Grijalva, Connolly, Green, Sherrill, Greene, LaMalfa, Gonzales, Swalwell, Cherfilus-McCormick, Mullin, Waltz). 5 seats are TBD/uncalled; 120th results are media calls, uncertified; 120th leadership is TBD (elections Nov 18–19, 2026).
  • Opposition credit is conservative: race = cycle + office + state + district + election phase (primary / general / other, from the filing's ele_type — expenditure dates are blank on UDP rows, so no date split is possible); credit only when UDP backed exactly one candidate in that phase of the race.

Sources & reproducibility

FEC bulk files are the source of truth for every figure; the FEC API was used for spot-checks; OpenSecrets is a cross-check only. The full method, reconciliation tables, and every limitation are in methodology.md. The pipeline that builds this dataset is scripted (python3 receipts/rebuild.py) and reproduces the committed dataset byte-identically; this page is generated from that dataset (plus the race-level opposition-credit record in data/intermediate/opposition-computed.json, shown in the drill-down) by receipts/site/build_site.py — never hand-edited.
